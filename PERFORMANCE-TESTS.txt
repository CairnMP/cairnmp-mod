CAIRNMP - LOCAL PERFORMANCE CAPTURE

Performance diagnostics are opt-in. With Cairn closed, set Debug.PerformanceDiagnostics
to true in UserData/MelonPreferences.cfg. Leave NativeProfiling false initially and
JobWorkerCount at 0 to retain the engine's worker configuration. PerformanceHudLanguage
supports en (default) and pt-BR. No capture starts automatically and no data is uploaded.

SOLO FIRST
1. Open Cairn manually through Steam and enter Story directly. Play the route where
   the stutter occurs; a multiplayer partner and the solo rope-test hotkeys are not needed.
2. Press Ctrl+F8 once. The status overlay shows a 30-second warmup and then three
   minutes of recording. Continue playing normally with the same graphics settings.
3. Wait for SAVED (complete). The result stays visible. Ctrl+F8 during a recording
   stops it early and produces a partial result; it is not a completed three-minute run.
4. Reports are saved locally under UserData/CairnMultiplayer/Performance as JSON and
   Markdown. Keep the build and scenario information with the report.

The next comparison can use the same route/settings in a private Belay Team room
without a partner. This measures idle multiplayer overhead, not an active pair,
remote avatars or the belayer's rope drawing. Those require matched two-player runs.

MEASUREMENT SCOPE
- The frame interval is the time between CairnMP OnUpdate callbacks. It is not a
  display-present measurement and does not separately identify CPU and GPU time.
- TimingScopeRevision 2 includes local belay and diagnostics in ModUpdate. Ropes
  includes the local belay adapter and the direct harness tether. Older scope
  revisions omitted that local belay work; do not treat their ModUpdate values as
  the same measurement when comparing results.
- UI includes OnGUI calls between Updates. ModUpdate and subsystem scopes overlap;
  do not sum every area as if they were independent parts of a frame.
- Native callbacks outside instrumented scopes, background transport/voice work,
  GPU time and uninstrumented game work remain unavailable. Stopwatch scopes measure
  elapsed wall time, including synchronous native calls and preemption, not CPU usage.
- Scene/session transitions are retained. Focus and time scale are sampled once per
  second during capture. Loading, pauses and focus changes can produce long intervals;
  they must be interpreted with context instead of silently discarded.
- The lightweight GameTuning log now retains finite positive gaps above five seconds.
  Its bounded rolling samples and Unity delta source are separate from the recorder's
  Stopwatch intervals.

THE VISUAL OPTIMIZATION
The belayer's remote drawing uses persistent playback/native point buffers and
managed interpolation, followed by one SetPositions call per update. It avoids
per-point Vector3.Lerp/constructor calls and renderer readbacks for blending.
Material, sample limits, topology identity, interpolation timing and stale drawing
expiry remain unchanged. This drawing is inactive in ordinary solo Story.

Allocation tests and fewer engine calls do not establish an FPS gain. Compare actual
captures before attributing stutter or improvement to the game, loader or mod. The
first solo capture requires no external profiler or system/graphics tuning changes.
The optional scripts/capture-performance.ps1 separately requires a supplied PresentMon
executable for external display measurements; it is not used by Ctrl+F8.

When sharing a report, review local paths and hardware metadata. NativeProfiling is
a separate opt-in probe with additional hooks; leave it off for this first capture.
